# M101 AC equipment baseline and environment guard — 2026-11-06

This is a read-only audit. No AC production source, scene, resource, user save, or live process was changed. `AC_BASELINE.json` records SHA-256 of current protected files and exact equipment/interaction function bodies. `ac_baseline_guard.py` compares later source state with this baseline and exits nonzero on any protected difference. Run `python review_artifacts/physical_instruments_20261106/ac_baseline_guard.py` before and after an M101 environment integration; it passed at capture. A same-hash result is source-level evidence only, not an engine/runtime outcome.

## Protected entry points
- `scripts/game.gd` extends `scripts/campus/performance/equipment_builder_adapter.gd`, which extends `scripts/equipment_builder.gd`. `_ready()` calls `_build_station()`, and `_build_room()` builds two additional AC stations. The first station's new children are reparented under `TrainingStation`, scaled by 0.68 and translated `(0,.75,-1)`. The two others are likewise scaled/translated at X ±6. The node hierarchy and transforms are protected.
- `scripts/equipment_builder.gd::_build_station` creates rack back, vertical rails, horizontal supports, then calls all trainer builders: `_make_ammeters`, `_make_wattmeters`, `_make_voltmeters`, three `_make_load` groups, `_make_daq`, `_make_switch_board`, `_make_transformer`, `_make_supply`, `_make_dynamometer`, `_make_motor`. All those functions and their helpers `_mat`, `_box`, `_cylinder`, `_sphere`, `_line`, `_text3`, `_make_module`, `_socket`, `_decorative_socket`, `_switch`, `_control`, `_meter`, `_face`, `_small_port`, `_ring`, `_machine` are protected. Equipment catalog, lab state/interaction model, performance builder/mesh cache are protected dependencies.
- Six panel art resources under `assets/panels/` are protected with their `.import` metadata: daq, inverter, transformer, supply, dynamo_guard, motor_guard. `_face` loads these SVGs into each existing panel. They are the actual AC device faces. Do not apply DC instrument meshes or theme colors to them.
- `_socket` creates `StaticBody3D` role `socket` with exact ID, label, collision and terminal geometry; IDs are appended to `terminal_ids` and indexed in `terminal_bodies`. `_switch` creates role `switch` and indexes `switch_meshes`; `_control` creates role `control` and indexes `control_bodies`. `game.gd` takes their global positions into `session.initialize`/`session.add_station`, and `_render_wires`, `_port_position`, `_render_stations`, `_sync_supply_controls`, `_physics_process`, `activate`, `_request`, `_input`, `toggle_inspection` use those identities. Preserving these transforms and maps is necessary for wiring, distance checks, interaction and inspection.

## Environment integration boundary
- The room owner can change M101 shell/floor/ceiling, furnishings, whiteboard/instructor area and lighting only through a separate environment entry point. Avoid a global material/mesh override or broad transform change affecting `TrainingStation` or the two spawned equipment nodes. A diff in `game.gd::_build_room` needs human review: the function contains both environment and AC station spawning; the baseline guard intentionally flags any change to it.
- `scripts/campus/presentation/decor/m101_geometry.patch` changes AC rack/support geometry in `_build_station`; `lab_floor_coplanar.patch` changes `equipment_builder.gd` environment geometry. `M101_BASELINE_RESTRICTION.md` explicitly withdraws both. Do not adopt either patch as a new M101 environment input. The current `equipment_builder.gd` already contains rack values `(0,2.28,-3.09)` size `(6.52,4.50,.19)`, vertical supports at Y 2.335 height 4.61, and bottom rail Y .055. These are now part of the frozen current baseline; no rollback was done.
- Survey of staged `.patch` files found the two above as the only direct references to `equipment_builder.gd`; no staged patch found that replaces an `assets/panels` resource or recolors AC modules globally. Source-only search does not prove every future integration is safe, hence the guard.

## Scope distinction
The earlier `assets/instruments/physical` and `scripts/instruments/physical/bench_instruments.gd` are for the separate DC station. Their staged shared hooks target `dc_lab.gd` and `game.gd` DC interaction hints, not the AC builder. This baseline task did not reopen or edit that feature.
